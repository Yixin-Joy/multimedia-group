Relative slowdown

Speed <50% of the same detector’s preceding-history median and positive volume. Requires ≥96 prior positive-volume buckets spanning ≥48 hours. Baseline uses up to the selected window + 72 hours of history, excludes future observations and is not time-of-day adjusted.

Definitions, sources & limitations

Network speed is the unweighted mean of valid detector speeds, after the existing pipeline’s volume-weighted aggregation of lane observations. Low speed: <20 km/h with volume >0. Current KPI freshness: latest observation within 15 minutes of the selected reference time. Coverage: fresh matching detectors / matching catalog detectors (full catalog: 807 in the supplied snapshot). Gray points are not counted as low speed.

The catalog aliases “Central and Western” and “Central & Western” are combined for display; original stored values remain unchanged. Other road-name variants are retained. Historical results use observed detector-buckets, with changing sensor coverage and potentially incomplete 15-minute intervals. Volume is a sum of sampled source counts, not a complete traffic census. Weekday/weekend and peak comparisons are descriptive, not causal or statistically representative.
